Assumptions
  • Dealer Amount = % × Total Loan Amount Financed; Broker Amount is any extra manual payout, added on top.
  • Effective outflow adds Total Payout (Dealer Amount + Broker Amount) to the Total Capitalised Loan Amount (Section 06 of the EMI tab).
  • EMI here is always computed on the capitalised amount, regardless of the EMI Type selected in the EMI Calculator tab.
  • Effective ROI is solved numerically (bisection) so that the same EMI stream, discounted at that rate, equals the effective outflow (capitalised loan + payout).
